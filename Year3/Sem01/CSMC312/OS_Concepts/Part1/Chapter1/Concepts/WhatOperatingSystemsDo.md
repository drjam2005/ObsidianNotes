# What Operating Systems Do
- Depends on ones point of view
- User wants convenience, **ease of use** and **good performance**
    - They don't care about optimal **resource utilization**
- But shared computers such as **mainframe** or **microcomputer** must keep all users happy
- Users of dedicated systems such as **workstations** have dedicated resources, but frequently use shared resources from servers
- Handheld computers are resource poor, optimized for usability and battery life
- Some computers have little to no user interface, such as embedded computers in devices and automobiles


## Takeaways
- certain computers are catered towards different kinds of usage, mainly depends on the user's objective

# The Foundation: The CIA Triad
- **Confidentiality**: Ensuring that information is acessible only to authorized individuals. It prevents individuals. It prevents unauthorized access to data, keeping it private and proteced.
    - **Control**: Encryption, MFA, access control.
    - **Threat**: Eavesdropping, data breaches, credential theft.
- Integrity: Ensuring that information is trustworthy and has not been altered, deleted, or midified by unauthorized people.
    - **Control**: Hashing (SHA-256), digital signatures
    - **Threat**: Man-in-the-middle malware, data interference.
- **Control**: Redundant servers, backups, DDoS protection.
    - **Threat**: DoS attacks, ransomware, hardware failure.

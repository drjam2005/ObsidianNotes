# What is Ethical Hacking?
- The practice of intentionally probing computer systems, networks, and applications to find security vulnerabilities that a malicious hacker could epxloit.
- The difference between a criminal hacker and an ethical hacker isn't the tools they use, it's **intention, permission, and authorization.**
